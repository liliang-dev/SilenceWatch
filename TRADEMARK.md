# Trademark policy

The SilenceWatch **source code** is open source, under the licences described in
the [README](README.md). The **name** "SilenceWatch" and the **logo** are not.

This is the usual arrangement for open-source products, and it exists for one
reason: so that "SilenceWatch" keeps meaning something specific. If anyone could
ship anything under the name, nobody could trust an instance because it said
SilenceWatch on it.

## What you may do without asking

- Run SilenceWatch, modified or not, for yourself or for your employer
- Say that your product "uses SilenceWatch", "is built on SilenceWatch", or "is
  compatible with SilenceWatch"
- Use the name in articles, talks, comparisons, tutorials and documentation
- Keep the name in an unmodified redistribution of the software
- Use the logo to link to the project

## What needs written permission

- Naming your product, service or company "SilenceWatch", or anything close
  enough to be confused with it
- Using the name or logo as the brand of a **modified** version — in particular
  offering a hosted service called SilenceWatch that is not this project's
- Registering domain names, social accounts, or app-store listings using the name
- Using the logo in a way that implies endorsement or affiliation

If you fork and modify the software, please give the fork its own name. You keep
every right the licence grants you over the code; you simply do not carry the name
with it.
