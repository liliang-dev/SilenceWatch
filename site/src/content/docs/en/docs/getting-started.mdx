This page takes you from nothing to a first monitored job with a working alert. Allow five minutes.

<Steps>

1. **Create an account** at [app.silencewatch.com](https://app.silencewatch.com/login). A first project is created with the account. Checks, alert channels and history belong to a project: you can create more (one per customer or per team, say) in *Settings › Projects*.

2. **Create a check.** In *Checks*, click *New check* and fill in:
   - the **name** ("Nightly backup");
   - the **schedule**, either "every N seconds" or a [cron expression](/en/docs/schedules-and-states/) with its time zone;
   - the **grace period**: how late a run may be before you are alerted;
   - an optional **environment** (`production`, `staging`) so jobs do not get mixed up.

3. **Copy the ping URL.** It is shown at the top of the check's page, with a ready-to-paste `curl` command. It is the only credential your job needs: keep it secret.

4. **Have your job call that URL** at the end of a successful run:

   ```bash
   /usr/local/bin/backup.sh && curl -fsS -m 10 --retry 3 https://app.silencewatch.com/p/<ping-key>
   ```

   For your environment (systemd, Docker, Kubernetes, GitHub Actions…), see the [examples](/en/docs/cron-examples/).

5. **Add an alert channel.** In *Alerting*, add an email address, a webhook, Slack, Microsoft Teams or Discord, then click *Send test*. A channel nobody has ever tried is a channel that fails during the incident it was set up for.

6. **Check it works.** The check is *waiting* until a first ping arrives, then goes *UP* on the first call. If a ping is missing it goes *late*, then *down* once the grace period has passed, and every enabled channel is alerted.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="Measuring duration">
Also call `/start` at the beginning of the job: SilenceWatch then measures how long each run takes. See the [ping API](/en/docs/ping-api/).
</Aside>
