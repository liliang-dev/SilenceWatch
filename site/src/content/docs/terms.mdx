## 1. The service

SilenceWatch monitors scheduled tasks (cron, workers, jobs) through heartbeats: HTTP calls they send when they run. When a heartbeat does not arrive in time, the service opens an incident and sends an alert to the channels you configured (email, webhook, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord).
