The subscription is monthly and renews automatically every month, at the same price, until you cancel it. Each payment is taken on the anniversary of the subscription date. An invoice is sent to you with every payment.

If a payment fails, Stripe retries it for a few days. If it still cannot be collected, the subscription ends and the account returns to the free plan (see section 7).
